Keep the image. Add a little life.

  1. Image detail: import a still image, choose a look, and set contour quality. Your image stays fixed.
  2. Layers: enable Trails, Whole image, Traveling dots and Shimmer dots independently. Solo isolates a layer in preview and export; click again to return to the mix.
  3. Direct the motion: place source areas and guides with Direct motion. Waypoints steer through, attractors pull in, and repellers push away within their visible range. Guides affect Trails, Whole image and Traveling dots, with optional attractor/repeller response in Shimmer dots. Paths still stop at image gaps. Seed + Shuffle changes the arrangement, and Master speed slows everything together.
  4. Export: name your shot, choose the view and seconds, then Render MP4. The visible summary shows the view, background, solo state, resolution and fixed 24 fps. Use preview view copies what you are viewing.

Image on/off changes only the preview background. Presets preserve image, points, masks, seed and shot settings. Undo is available after a preset or Reset all. Full preview preserves appearance; Draft is only for rough timing.
